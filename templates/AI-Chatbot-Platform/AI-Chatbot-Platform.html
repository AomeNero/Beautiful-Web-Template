How It Works

Get your AI assistant up and running in three simple steps.

Step 1: Choose Your Model

  • Select from GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, or custom fine-tuned models
  • Configure system prompts and personality traits
  • Set response parameters like temperature and max tokens

Step 2: Design Your Interface

  • Customize colors, fonts, and layout to match your brand
  • Add rich media support — images, files, and interactive elements
  • Configure conversation flows and fallback responses

Step 3: Deploy Anywhere

Think of it like reading a book: a regular computer reads one page at a time, while our AI processes entire knowledge bases simultaneously to give you instant, accurate answers.
